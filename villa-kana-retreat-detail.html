A two-story villa tucked behind a quiet lane in Seminyak, built around a private infinity pool that looks out over open rice fields. The open-plan living and dining area connects straight to the pool deck, and the fully equipped kitchen makes it easy to cook for the whole group. Upstairs, two of the three bedrooms open onto a shared terrace with the same rice-field view.

The villa sits a 7-minute drive from Seminyak's beach clubs and restaurants, but far enough from the main strip to stay quiet at night. Our housekeeping team visits daily, and a dedicated host is reachable by WhatsApp throughout your stay for anything you need.
